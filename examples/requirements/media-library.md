# Lesson Library

A small learning site for a painting school. Courses hold short video lessons: each lesson has a video (MP4 or WebM),
a poster image, its length and a topic, and caption tracks in English and Japanese that viewers switch between while
watching. The school also runs a podcast: episodes are audio recordings with show notes, a transcript file, their
length in minutes and a series (interviews, lessons, news); listeners keep an episode playing while they browse.

Visitors browse lessons by their picture, filter them by topic and open a lesson to watch it with captions. The
podcast reads newest first. Staff upload the videos, posters, audio files and subtitle files from the app, and the
length of a video or an episode fills itself in from the file.

Design: clean and quiet, the video first; minimal or editorial looks.
