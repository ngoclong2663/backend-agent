# Lantern Audio — a small podcast network

Lantern Audio produces a handful of shows. Producers manage the shows and their episodes; listeners browse the
network and listen on the site.

## What the app holds

- **Shows**: a name, a description, cover art (a square image), a category (culture, science, history, business,
  comedy) and a host name.
- **Episodes** belong to a show: a title, show notes (a long text), the audio file, a transcript file (a subtitle
  file people can read along), the duration in minutes (filled from the uploaded audio), an episode number, a
  publish date and a status: a producer creates an episode as a draft, then publishes it; a published episode can be
  taken down (unpublished).
- **Guests**: a name, a short bio and a photo; an episode can have several guests and a guest appears in several
  episodes.

## People

**Producers** sign in to manage shows, episodes and guests and to publish. Anyone, signed in or not, can browse the
shows and listen to published episodes; drafts are never public.

## Look

Warm and paper-like: an off-white background with ruled lines, serif headings set like a magazine, a deep green
accent, generous space. Cover art first when browsing shows; episodes read like a listing with the player at hand.
